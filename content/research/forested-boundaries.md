# Forested boundaries and two-sided exploration

## Problem

Start with a forested boundary arc and explore it from its two ends. The goal is to identify the conditional law of the unexplored middle surface and encode its boundary lengths by Lévy processes.

## Working picture

- The forested boundary is represented by a Poissonian collection of quantum disks attached along a spine.
- One-sided exploration exposes a stationary independent-increment structure.
- The two-sided statement requires care about what is genuinely independent and what is merely a time reversal of the same sampled object.
- A useful endpoint is a boundary-length process started from its initial length and run until the relevant hitting time.
