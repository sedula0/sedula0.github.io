# Planar maps for radial SLE on LQG

## Question

Can one identify a natural planar-map model whose scaling limit gives radial SLE drawn on an LQG surface, with a discrete exploration that exposes the correct continuum boundary-length data?

## Why it is interesting

A convincing model should make the radial target point visible in the discrete geometry rather than adding it only after taking a continuum limit. Ideally, its encoding walk would also explain why the appropriate SLE/LQG coupling appears.

## Things to test

- candidate decorated planar maps with a distinguished interior target;
- exploration procedures whose boundary data have a tractable walk encoding;
- relationships with whole-plane/radial reversibility constructions;
- whether known mating-of-trees bijections admit a radial analogue without forcing unnatural boundary conditions.
