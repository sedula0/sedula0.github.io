# Stable looptrees as the index varies

## Question

How continuously can stable looptrees be coupled as the stability index varies? A particularly attractive formulation would place the random metric-measure spaces on one probability space and control their Gromov–Hausdorff or Gromov–Hausdorff–Prokhorov distance as the parameter changes.

## Possible route

A direct approach would try to couple the coding Lévy excursions first, then propagate quantitative control through the looptree quotient construction. The hard part is making the coding continuity strong enough near large jumps, where the geometry changes visibly.
